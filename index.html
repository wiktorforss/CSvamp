How the score works

A heuristic 0–100 score, not a guarantee. It combines: rain over the last 2–4 weeks (fungi fruit 1–3 weeks after soaking rain), 7-day mean air temperature, soil temperature (6 cm), relative humidity and soil moisture, with a penalty for frost and a seasonal curve peaking Aug–Oct. Forecast days use model forecasts, so the further ahead, the less certain.
